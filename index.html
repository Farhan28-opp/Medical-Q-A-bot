About MedAI

A document-grounded medical knowledge assistant

MedAI is designed to answer general health questions by retrieving relevant passages from a curated medical knowledge base and generating an educational response from that material.

Because every answer is tied to source documents, you can review where the information came from rather than relying on an unexplained response.

AI-generated answers can still be incomplete or imprecise. MedAI is intended to support learning and understanding, not to diagnose conditions or replace guidance from a licensed healthcare professional.
